Privacy Policy for Block Sort

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Block Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Oaklogix ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

1. No account, no personal data collected by us

Block Sort does not require an account or sign-in. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information on our servers.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, daily puzzle streak, settings such as sound) is saved only on your device using local storage (shared preferences). It is never uploaded by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads, including optional rewarded ads, using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads. Please see the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android).

4. Children

Block Sort is a general audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no account data that can be exposed. Third-party services such as AdMob apply their own security measures.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
